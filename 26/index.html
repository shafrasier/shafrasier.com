01 · THE NIGHT

Twenty-six years in, one thing has held: everything I love about music routes back to the dance floor. Disco is where the roads meet — the strings and the drum machines, Philadelphia and Munich, the loft and the garage.

So for my birthday we walk the whole territory in one night: the seventies records that started it, and the Euro, Italo, hi-NRG and electro forms they became when the lights changed. One room, one ball, seven hours.

There will be alcohol. Bring more if you like — or whatever else moves you. And bring your people: the floor is big, and the invitation travels.
