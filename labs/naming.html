Named after someone

The Claude model: name the thing after the person whose work is its conceptual foundation. For this project the candidates cluster into three kinds — the people who made music transmissible, the people who tried to map culture, and the people who built connection machines before the technology existed.

One practical filter before you fall in love with any of them. A coined word buys ownership — a clean domain, no collision, nothing to disambiguate — and costs recognition: every visitor has to be taught what it means, and you will spend the rest of the project's life explaining it. An eponym splits the difference: unfamiliar at first glance but instantly meaningful once explained, and the explanation is the pitch. "Atlas" was the opposite trade — instantly legible, impossible to own.
